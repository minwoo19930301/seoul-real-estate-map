# Civic silhouette module

`scripts/civic_facade.py` exports `civic_geometry(mesh, poly, name, height, floors, seed) -> bool`. The caller owns selection, source matching, fallback heights, model preservation, and publication. Unknown names and invalid input return `False` before adding vertices. The module uses the existing three Mesh material batches and imports no builder code. It does not write files.

## Bounds and material contract

- The supplied positive `height` is a hard upper bound. Every building starts at local y=0; no foundation below the plane and no roof plant above the envelope.
- Footprint scale/orientation comes from the actual input polygon. Timber bodies, roofs, columns, courtyard cuts and major office massing are clipped to it. Small glazing offsets (6 mm), surface ribs (up to 16 mm vertically, clamped to height), and clock-face layers avoid coplanar flicker; they are display details, not new surveyed dimensions.
- Roof and curve samples are deterministic. Roof colors vary slightly by tile course; company detail choice is seed-stable. All normals come from the existing Mesh cross-product normalization.
- Material 0: opaque mineral, timber, fins and structure. Material 1: window/glass panels. Material 2: roof/caps. Maximum three draw calls per built GLB.
- Names are matched after stripping whitespace. Corporate tokens cover selected Samsung, SK, LG, Hyundai, Hanwha, Lotte, POSCO, Doosan, CJ, GS, KT, Kyobo, Naver and Kakao names; unknown names return `False` so the integration can use its generic fallback.

## Verification scope

The module was exercised with 13 supported names against axis-aligned, rotated, concave and courtyard polygons (52 combinations), plus an unknown-name no-op. These checks cover finite coordinates, finite unit normals, y=0 bases and no height-envelope overflow. They do not certify visual similarity of every selected source building. No full builder or global manifest mutation was performed by this modeling subtask.


## Actual 흥인지문 footprint refinement

The source outline was projected to metres and visually inspected on 2026-09-19. Its full oriented bounds are approximately 52.90 × 43.50 m; this includes the open horseshoe barbican and adjoining straight stone base. A pavilion covering those full bounds would be incorrect.

For double-tier gates with source area / oriented bounding rectangle area below 0.72, `_pavilion_core` searches rectangles entirely covered by the polygon. Search width and depth are capped at 60% and 35% of the full oriented spans, then progressively reduced until a covered rectangle is found. Placement favours the thick portion of the source stone base. This is a bounded approximate inscribed-rectangle search, not a claim of survey-derived pavilion position or mathematically exact maximum area.

For this actual 흥인지문 source it selects an approximately **28.409 × 13.626 m** roof envelope, wholly within the straight gate base. The lower/upper timber masses and both roof tiers are inside that envelope; the arch tunnel shares its axis. Original ground-plan masonry, horseshoe opening, and the overall 20 m official-reference model height are retained. Arch width, masonry elevation, pavilion setbacks, roof pitch, and exact placement are still modeling estimates.

Verification used the actual selected source polygon at 20 m, not a rectangular fixture: 4,538 triangles, finite coordinates and unit normals, y-min 0, y-max 20 m. Every vertex above the 8.7 m masonry/coping datum lies inside the chosen pavilion rectangle. Two depth-buffered software views were rendered and inspected at `/tmp/heunginjimun-actual-preview.png`; the inscribed footprint overlay is `/tmp/heunginjimun-core.png`. These temporary views are modeling QA, not a browser/deployment claim.
