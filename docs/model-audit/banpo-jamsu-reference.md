## Geometry decisions and limits

- Upper bridge: retain **the entire source polygon including northern viaduct and both approach branches**. The source's3m height tag is deck depth metadata, not total bridge height. Upper typical crossing supports use the public30m rhythm, paired legs with face ribs/recesses, haunched headers, four pale steel boxes and diaphragms observed in the photos. The main road, safety rail and curved lamp silhouettes are separate components.
- Lower bridge:18m wide slab centered below the upper deck, independently modeled steel plate/I sections,15m ordinary support rhythm, and missing intermediate lower piers in the raised navigation segment. The raised roadway is a smooth longitudinal crest rather than an arch truss above the roadway. Red cycleway, green pedestrian strip, curbs and railings follow the crest.
- The795m official length describes the river crossing. The modeled lower route includes landward approaches extending beyond it; its approximately955m total does **not** claim an official bridge length of955m. The narrow OSM lower polygon is not accepted as a complete width survey: official18m width and stacked alignment take precedence.
- **Estimated, not measured:** absolute upper/lower elevations19.4m/5.35m above local asset datum,5.7m crest rise,240m crest extent, crest station, column/cap/girder cross sections, lamp spacing, lane allocations, end-span placement, and ramp grade. The public diagram establishes typical rhythms, not every current pier center or an exact total count. A CODIL inventory search result was found but its PDF could not be retrieved reliably; no exact pier count is claimed from it.
- Northern ramp/deck branches are retained at the plan extent, but their substructure is incomplete because inspected photos do not show a defensible support schedule. That limitation is visible in the full overview and must not be described as a complete structural reconstruction.
- Authoring coordinates: eastX/northY/upZ, anchor126.996466/37.514546. Standard GLB converts to eastX/upY/southZ; minimum vertical coordinate0. Normalization bakes transforms without recentering.
